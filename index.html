Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17/09/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Milestones ("the app") handles information when you use the app.

Milestones is developed and operated by Davis Miyashiro.

Information You Provide

Milestones allows you to create and manage projects, tasks, reminders, due dates, and related information.

Information that you enter into the app is stored locally on your device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

The app does not currently require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Analytics Information

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help understand how the app is used and to improve its features and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect information about interactions with the app and technical information about the app and device. Depending on the platform, configuration, and Firebase services in use, this may include:

  • app interactions and events;
  • app version and application identifiers;
  • device and operating system information;
  • Firebase installation or related technical identifiers;
  • general usage and engagement information; and
  • other analytics information collected according to Google's applicable policies.

Analytics information is used to understand aggregate usage patterns, evaluate app features, and improve the app. I do not sell your personal information.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to identify crashes, errors, and technical problems and to improve the reliability of the app.

When a crash or other diagnostic event occurs, Firebase Crashlytics may automatically collect technical information including:

  • crash reports and stack traces;
  • relevant application state at the time of a crash;
  • app version and application identifiers;
  • device model and technical device information;
  • operating system name and version;
  • Crashlytics and Firebase installation identifiers;
  • the time at which a crash or diagnostic event occurred; and
  • diagnostic logs or other technical information associated with the crash.

This information is used for crash reporting, debugging, and improving the stability and performance of the app.

Firebase and Google

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are services provided by Google. Information collected by these services is processed according to Google's and Firebase's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention

Projects, tasks, reminders, and other information stored locally by Milestones remain on your device until they are removed through the app, the app's data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, depending on the type of information.

Analytics and diagnostic information processed by Firebase is retained according to Google's and Firebase's applicable retention policies and the configuration of those services.

I do not maintain a separate server-side user account containing your locally stored Milestones projects, tasks, reminders, or other locally stored app information.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the app. Communications with third-party services are transmitted using secure network connections where supported by those services.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

You can remove locally stored Milestones information by using available app controls, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, as applicable.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information processed about you, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how your personal information is processed.

For questions or requests relating to information controlled by Milestones, you can contact me using the email address below.

Information processed independently by Google or Firebase may also be subject to the privacy controls and rights mechanisms provided by those services.

Links to Other Websites and Services

The app may contain links to third-party websites or services. When you follow a third-party link, you are interacting with a service that I do not operate. I am not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties, and you should review their privacy policies where appropriate.

Children's Privacy

Milestones is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

I do not knowingly request or collect directly identifying personal information from children through the app. If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to me, please contact me so that I can investigate and, where appropriate, delete that information.

Third-party services used by the app may process technical and analytics information as described in this Privacy Policy and according to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when features are added, third-party services change, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where appropriate, significant changes may also be communicated through the app.
